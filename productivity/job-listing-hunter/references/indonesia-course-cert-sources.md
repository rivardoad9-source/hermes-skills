## Paid but career-relevant (accounting/tax track)

- **Brevet Pajak A & B** — the most-requested item in Indonesian "staff akuntansi / tax" ads, open
  to any major. Comparators: `taxacademy.id` ≈ Rp1,75jt per module online, offline batch ≈ Rp3,5jt;
  `kelasbrevet.com` runs flash sales (registered training institution, Jakarta office).
- **IAI Basic Financial Accounting (BFA)** — verified price at IAI Jakarta: **Rp3.200.000 virtual /
  Rp4.400.000 in person for non-members** (members Rp2,7jt/3,85jt); ends with the IAI competency exam.
  IAI Jatim runs an online evening cohort (Tue & Thu 19.00–21.00 WIB) that fills slowly — check the
  live course list for the current batch.
- **CAFB** (IAI foundation level, adapted from ICAEW CFAB) — `web.iaiglobal.or.id/Sertifikasi-IAI`.
- Professional titles (CPA/CA Indonesia, US-CGAA/CGAE) need an accounting education path and cost
  millions — do NOT recommend them to a non-accounting undergrad in semester 5.

## Semester-long (semester 6/7, convert to SKS — cannot run beside regular classes)

- **Magang Berdampak** (formerly MSIB): 1 semester internship, ~20 SKS conversion, stipend up to
  ~Rp2,8jt/month. The 2026 batch registration closed mid-2026 → watch
  `simbelmawa.kemdiktisaintek.go.id` for the next one.
- **MagangHub (Kemnaker)** — national internship program, opened per batch/angkatan.
- **Bangkit Academy** — free, 900+ hours / 20 weeks, Google-linked certificate + SKS conversion;
  eligibility: S1 **semester ≥5 while the program runs**, full-time (no concurrent job/internship).
- **Wirausaha Merdeka (WMM)** — 1 semester, free + business funding, SKS conversion.

## Eligibility traps to state out loud

1. Government free-certification grants (e.g. Kemdiktisaintek competence/professional certification
   for students) target **vokasi D3/D4** — an S1 student is usually ineligible. Don't assume; tell
   the user to ask their prodi.
2. Programs with a **Commitment Letter / no-other-activity clause** (Bangkit, Magang Berdampak, WMM)
   cannot be taken alongside regular classes.
3. Many "magang" programs require the campus to have a mandatory internship course (PKL) on the KRS —
   the registration asks for an official campus letter + KTM + KRS.
4. Always tell the user to ask the prodi/career centre about campus-partnered certification quotas
   (SAP/Accurate/Microsoft) — those are frequently free or subsidised and are not advertised online.
