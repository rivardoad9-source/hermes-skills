# Factory outreach — physical product (Indonesia)

Use after a product passes the demand test and before paying for a batch. The goal of the first message is a quote and a sample, not a relationship.

## First WhatsApp message

```text
Selamat malam, saya [nama]. Saya sedang menyiapkan brand [kategori produk] untuk [channel: studio/toko/online]. Volume awal [24–60] unit per desain, dan akan naik kalau ada repeat order.

Boleh minta info:
1. Harga per unit untuk 24, 60, dan 150 unit.
2. MOQ per desain dan per warna.
3. Bahan dan kadar elastane/komposisi.
4. Spesifikasi grip/konstruksi yang tersedia.
5. Bisa logo bordir atau rajut? Biaya setup dan format file yang dibutuhkan?
6. Lead time sampel dan produksi.
7. Ukuran yang tersedia.
8. Biaya sampel 1 unit dan ongkir ke [kota].
9. Pembayaran DP 50% atau full di depan?

Kalau ada foto atau video hasil produksi yang sudah jadi, boleh dikirim. Terima kasih.
```

## Sample request

Ask for a blank sample first. Test before designing:

• Wash 10× with ordinary detergent, no bleach, shade dry: fast screen.
• Wash 30×: final standard.
• Grip loss under 20%, shrinkage under 5%, no colour bleed, seams intact, elastic recovers.
• Try the sample on the actual surface the user will use (tile, reformer, mat).

## Red flags

• Only accepts a personal bank account, no PT/CV invoice.
• Cannot give a factory address or refuses a video call from the production floor.
• Quote is suspiciously below material cost; expect a thinner or recycled yarn.
• MOQ per colour is not stated; three colours can silently triple the order.
• Sample photos are catalogue images, not the factory’s own output.

## Follow-up

If no reply in 24 hours, send one short message: “Boleh saya minta quote untuk 24 dan 60 unit, plus biaya sampel? Saya butuh untuk perbandingan minggu ini.” Do not send more than one follow-up before moving to the next factory.
