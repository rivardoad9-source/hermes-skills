# Jasa pembukuan (retainer) — packages, SOP, protection

Delivery design for the bookkeeping offer. Market facts and prices live in
`indonesia-sme-market-benchmarks.md`; this file is HOW the service is run. Assumption check on client #1: measure
actual hours and replace the estimate below.

## Package tiers (prices = ID market anchors, re-verify before quoting)

Charge from **transaction count + accounts + who carries the liability**, never from hours.

1. **Rapi Dasar — Rp750rb/bulan** (first client Rp500rb, buying testimonial + permission to use it as a sample).
   1 bank account, ≤100 transaksi/bulan, no tax scope. Includes: recording from statements + receipts → jurnal &
buku besar · bank reconciliation · Laba Rugi + Arus Kas + Neraca sederhana · rekap penjualan · archived evidence
folder.
2. **Plus Pajak — Rp1,5jt/bulan.** Tier 1 + rekap PPh 21/23 & PPN in/out · prepares e-faktur & ID billing ·
reminder tanggal 15. **The client pays and files; never you.**
3. **Beresin Rampai — Rp1,5–3jt per backlogged month, minimum Rp1,5jt, once-off.** Reconstruct from bank statements
+ receipts, rebuild the COA, reconcile and surface the differences, produce the missing months, attach a page
listing what could not be verified. This is the tier that sells to accounting firms as sub-contract work.

**Add-ons (price them, don't absorb them):** >100 transaksi +Rp150rb per 50 · extra bank account +Rp100rb ·
stok/opname +Rp250rb · extra branch +Rp300rb · payroll/BPJS +Rp300rb/bulan.

## Monthly SOP with dates (the dates are the product)

- **Tgl 1–3 intake.** Request: bank statements (CSV/PDF), every receipt/invoice, cash evidence, sales report
  (POS/IG/WA/Shopee), closing stock, new AR/AP. Send a list of anything missing **the same day** — never wait until
the 5th.
- **Tgl 2–4 process.** Statement lines → journal rows → COA mapping → receipt-to-statement matching → reconcile
  until the difference is **0 or explained in writing** → stock vs purchases/sales check.
- **Tgl 4–5 QC then send** (checklist below).
- **Tgl 10 tax pack** (tier 2): PPh/PPN recap, e-faktur + ID billing ready for the client to pay before tanggal 15.
- **Tgl 25 follow-up.** "Ada nota yang belum masuk? Ada belanja besar bulan depan?" — this is what retains the
  client and surfaces add-on work.

**Contract clause that must exist:** data received by tanggal 3 → report on tanggal 5; late data moves the report
by 3 working days per late submission. Without it, the client's delay becomes your fault.

## Onboarding questionnaire (once per client)

1. Business name, sector, employees, branches? 2. Entity: perorangan / CV / PT; NPWP; PKP or non-PKP?
3. Monthly revenue + approximate transaction count. 4. Which banks/accounts are used for the business?
5. Where do they record today (book, Excel, Accurate, Mekari, POS)? 6. Who records now — will they keep helping
with input or hand it all over? 7. Which reports are *mandatory* for them (tax? bank? to know profit?)
8. Any backlog, how many months? 9. Which channel and which date can they commit to sending data?
10. Tax account + filing status (tier 2). 11. PIC name + reachable number for discrepancies.
12. What may you access, and what is off-limits (e.g. mobile banking) — plus a confidentiality note.

## Pre-send QC checklist (skip none)

1. Total debit = total credit. 2. Bank reconciliation difference 0 (else a written explanation sheet).
3. No negative cash/bank balance unless the story is real. 4. Year-to-date profit consistent with last month, or
jump explained. 5. Adjusting entries (depreciation, accrual) carry a date and a basis. 6. PPN rate correct and
input/output not swapped. 7. AR checked for items past due and uncollected. 8. Archive complete, filenames clean.
9. **The WhatsApp summary numbers equal the PDF numbers** — generate both from one source, never retype.

## Always out of scope — write it into the quote

Chasing evidence from third parties · audit/assurance or legal work · **signing or filing the client's tax
returns** · investment decisions · payroll/BPJS unless bought as an add-on · holding the client's banking
credentials.

## The retainer is kept by the summary, not the report

Every package ships a one-page plain-language summary (omzet, laba, kas, 3 things worth suspecting) plus answers to
3 business questions the owner asked. Micro clients renew because they can ask questions, not because they receive
statements.

## Capacity & revenue math (say this honestly)

Automated: 1 micro client ≈ 3–5 h/month → 6–8 clients solo ≈ 25–35 h/month. 5 × Rp750rb = Rp3,75jt/month;
3 tier-2 + 2 tier-1 = Rp6jt/month. Reaching Rp100jt needs ~50 clients, so this is **base income + proof + network
+ raw material for a productized version** — not the target path by itself. Raise prices with the 4th client onward;
never raise on an existing one.
