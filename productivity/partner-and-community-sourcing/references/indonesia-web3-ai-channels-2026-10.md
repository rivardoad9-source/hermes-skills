## Notes on reading this ecosystem

- Luma (`luma.com/<id>`) is the de facto event platform for this ecosystem; every useful event page carries JSON-LD with dates, host, attendance and prize, and body text with the timeline. Read it with `scripts/read_rendered_page.sh`.
- Hackathon Telegram groups are mentioned only in page footers/support widgets, never in search results. Always scrape `t.me/` links off the official site.
- Instagram and LinkedIn carry the official registration announcements (and the corrected deadlines) for Indonesian programmes; news aggregators lag by about a day and sometimes get the close date wrong.
